A single sentence of horror for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Chill** — one line, under 200 characters, meant to frighten a reader in one breath.

> The baby monitor crackled, and in my own voice, it whispered my daughter's name from the empty crib.

## How it works

`main.ts` assembles the line from its three clauses — the crackle, the voice, the empty crib — validates it against the competition gate (1–200 characters, a single sentence on one line, no trailing newline), and writes it byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

The entry is 100 characters, well inside the 200-character gate.

## Why this line

The scare works on three turns in one breath: a familiar household sound (the monitor crackling), an impossible detail (the voice is the parent's own), and the final image that reframes everything (the crib is empty). Nothing is announced as scary — the dread comes from what the sentence withholds: who is speaking, and where the daughter is.
